Boarding Illness Protocol
Tier 1 — Keep an eye on it. Minor changes (slight appetite dip, mild lethargy). Note it, monitor through the next check, no notification needed yet.
Tier 2 — Let the parent know. Something worth mentioning (soft stool, minor limp). Email the supervisor and Hospital Manager for a written record, and keep monitoring.
Tier 3 — Doctor should see this today. Vomiting, ongoing lethargy, refusing food/water. Notify the on-site or on-call doctor and the Hospital Manager right away.
Tier 4 — Emergency. Seizure, collapse, difficulty breathing, suspected bloat. Stabilize immediately; see seizure/ER guidance below.

Seizure protocol

Known seizure history: follow the pet's individual care plan on file. Time the seizure, keep the area clear, notify supervisor and Hospital Manager immediately.
No known history: doctor stabilizes the pet immediately; diagnostics and further treatment hold pending parent contact and payment approval, unless the situation is life-threatening.

Internet Outage

1. Reset the router and check cables. 2. Check the provider's outage map before submitting a ticket. 3. Submit a Thrive IT ticket if the outage isn't resolved or isn't on the outage map.
